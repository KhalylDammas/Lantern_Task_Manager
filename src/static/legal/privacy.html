Privacy Policy — Lantern Task Manager

Audience: Lantern Systems employees using the internal LTM Teams bot.

Data we process

  • Microsoft Entra ID identity (object ID, display name, email, department) from Teams sign-in
  • Task records you create or are assigned (descriptions, due dates, status, audit trail)
  • Chat messages sent to the bot to fulfill task-management requests
  • Optional read-only lookups from Dynamics 365 when you reference ERP entities
  • Operational telemetry (App Service logs, optional Application Insights)

How data is used

Data is used solely to operate task lifecycle workflows inside Lantern Systems: assignment, reminders, verification, and reporting. LLM providers (Groq and/or OpenAI/Azure OpenAI) process chat content to interpret requests; prompts are governed by internal redaction and policy settings.

Storage and retention

Task data is stored in Lantern-controlled cloud databases (Turso/SQL). Retention follows internal IT policy. When a task is closed and verified, records remain for audit purposes unless removed under a documented retention schedule.

Sharing

Data is not sold. It is shared only with Lantern Systems systems required to run the service (Microsoft 365, Azure, approved LLM APIs, and read-only D365 OData). It is not published to the public internet.

Your choices

Use of LTM is subject to company policy. For access or data questions, contact it@lanternsystems.com.
