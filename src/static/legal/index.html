Lantern Task Manager (LTM)

LTM is an internal Microsoft Teams bot for Lantern Systems staff. It helps create, assign, track, and verify operational tasks. Work itself is performed by people outside the bot; Dynamics 365 Finance & Operations is used for read-only lookups only.

This application is for authorized Lantern Systems employees only. It is installed as a personal (1:1) Teams app, not for team channels or group chats.
