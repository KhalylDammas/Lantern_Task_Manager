Terms of Use — Lantern Task Manager

Audience: Authorized Lantern Systems employees.

Internal use only

LTM is provided for Lantern Systems business operations. You must be an authorized employee with a valid Microsoft 365 account in the Lantern tenant. Do not share access or use the bot for personal non-business purposes.

No ERP transactions

LTM tracks operational tasks; it does not post transactions to Dynamics 365 or replace approved ERP workflows. D365 data shown in chat is read-only reference material.

Acceptable use

You are responsible for the accuracy of task information you submit. Company acceptable-use and information-security policies apply. Do not enter secrets, credentials, or regulated data beyond what is required for task tracking.

Availability

LTM is provided on a best-effort basis for internal staff. There is no external service-level agreement. Planned maintenance or outages may occur without notice.
